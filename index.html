What actually happened

Israel Canada Hotels (TASE: ISCN) assumed the leases on 8 Greek properties, ~1,076 rooms, for total consideration of only ~₪100–112M — mostly by assumption of debt, not cash. Greek landlords are Aroundtown and WH Europe. This is a lease deal, not a property purchase.

Sources: Ered.gr, Calcalist, Hotel Report

Why the economics matter to you

Lease liabilities of ~₪856.5M, mostly CPI-linked, are fixed. Rent cannot be renegotiated away. That leaves exactly three levers for anyone trying to move EBITDA this year: rate, ancillary/F&B capture, and occupancy from owned demand. That's your entire pitch, structurally.

Source: DeepTASE analysis

Leon's role, and his own words

Avigad retains a strong operating role specifically "so that the consistency, quality and experience that characterize Brown Hotels are ensured." At a Calcalist conference he described sourcing the best local Greek designers, architects and chefs, and called boutique hospitality visible "in the design, the food, the calm energy."

Sources: OT.gr, Calcalist, InBusinessNews Cyprus

The content-efficiency gap

Casa Cook earns ~322 followers per post (260K / 808 posts). Brown Hotels earns ~27 per post (39K / 1,427 posts) while following 3,928 accounts — a follow-for-follow growth pattern, not organic pull. That's roughly a 12x efficiency gap against the closest design-hotel comparable.

Sources: Casa Cook, Brown Hotels

No Greek demand hub, no F&B pillar

The corporate Greek Facebook page sits at 103 followers, zero reviews, and went ~5 weeks dark in peak season. Its only dining post drew 1 reaction. Casa Cook, by contrast, runs dedicated "Gastronomy" and "Beverages" pillars on its profile as a matter of brand structure.

Sources: Facebook, Casa Cook

✓

5 huge win opportunities

1
Convert the Play hotels' F&B void into high-margin ancillary revenue

Play Paros currently sends guests next door for breakfast, yet its à-la-carte breakfast already scores 10.0 on Booking.com. A low-capex branded breakfast + rooftop/beach-bar module across the three Play hotels captures spend that's currently walking out the door — right as Play Theatrou adds 50 rooms in September 2026.

Sources: Booking.com, OT.gr
2
Fix the buffet and beverage supply chain — buy back 0.5–0.8 stars fast

Corinthia's negatives are almost entirely fixable operations: par-stock discipline, a holding-temperature protocol, a 14-day menu cycle, one additional peak-hour bar FTE. Cheapest rate-defence available on a Hilton-affiliated soft-brand asset.

Source: TripAdvisor — Corinthia
3
Re-architect all-inclusive from extraction to generosity-with-upsell

Free water and coffee in the base tier; a paid premium dining tier as a bookable upgrade instead of a resented exclusion. Reverses the "rip-off" narrative and is the fastest route to positive EBITDA that doesn't require renegotiating rent.

Source: DeepTASE — lease economics
4
Own the kosher-Mediterranean F&B niche properly

Chalkida already runs a Mehadrin kosher resort with dual kitchens and rabbinic supervision, and Avigad has already proven the model in Cyprus with a dedicated chef. Current execution is criticized for lacking local character ("no Greek salad!"). A kosher-Greek culinary programme converts compliance into a marketable cuisine for the 20–25% Israeli guest mix management already targets.

Sources: Ynetnews, Jerusalem Post
5
Build the missing Greek demand engine — with food as the content pillar

Consolidate a single Brown/Play Greece hub, launch a TikTok/Reels-first food-and-rooftop content engine, add a UGC hashtag mechanic, and run a post-stay review-generation flow to close the review-volume gap. Villa Brown Ermou already proves Brown can hit 4.8 cleanliness / 4.9 location with a restaurant attached — use it as the internal proof case.

Sources: Casa Cook benchmark, Villa Brown Ermou
06

Walking in: the positioning

Lead with his own standard — never with the insolvency. Debt is his defensive ground; food quality is his stated pride, and therefore the most persuadable ground in the room.

Your service reputation is an asset. I know how to systemize it so it scales beyond individual staff excellence.

Where the guest promise is inconsistent — especially around F&B — I close the gap between expectation, execution, and monetization.

In a lease-heavy environment, I focus on the three things you can still move this year: margin, ancillary capture, and repeatable standards.

Open with

"I don't just run F&B. I increase total guest value by aligning service standards, outlet concepts, upsell design, and demand generation around the real guest journey."
