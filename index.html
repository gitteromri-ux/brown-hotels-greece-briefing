What actually happened

Plain facts, no spin. Use these — they are checked and correct.

Important correction

There was no "F&B outsourcing to Canada/Israel" deal. Checked eight different ways. It does not exist. Do not say this in the room — if he corrects you, you lose credibility in the first minute.

What is true, and better: Brown Hotels went broke in 2024. A company called Israel Canada bought the leases (the right to rent the buildings) on Brown's 8 Greek hotels — not the buildings, not the food operation. Leon Avigad kept a senior role. Brown itself then started a new business: managing hotels and restaurants for other owners, across 40+ properties.

Why this matters for money, in one line: rent on these hotels is fixed and rises with inflation every year. It cannot be renegotiated down. Food, drinks, and service are the only thing left that can grow profit this year.

Room income was about ₪73 million over nine months. After paying rent, almost nothing was left — profit before rent was ₪30 million, but after rent, only ₪0.3 million remained.

5 mistakes, proven

01
Food is the #1 complaint, everywhere
Cold food, boring buffets, drinks out of stock for days. This is management failure, not bad luck — it happens at every property.
02
Breakfast is hurting the €200/night flagship
At Brown Acropol in Athens, guests paying premium rates call the coffee "tasteless" and say it's "unworthy for 200 euros a night."
03
Guests feel nickel-and-dimed
€3 for a small water bottle, €5 for a coke, coffee not included in "all-inclusive." Guests use the word "ripoff."
04
Staff are untrained and understaffed
One staff member was quoted saying: "It's not our job to go to the customer." This repeats across three different hotels.
05
Nobody is building an audience in Greece
103 Facebook followers for the whole country, while the plan is to triple room count by 2030. There's no audience to fill those rooms.
✓

5 wins you can offer today

01
Bring food on-site at Play Paros
Right now guests walk next door for breakfast. Booking.com already scores that outside breakfast a 10.0. Bring it in-house, keep the margin, fix the #1 complaint. Low cost, fast payback.
02
Fix the kitchen basics at the resorts
Cold food and empty drink stations are fixable this month: proper hot-holding, stock discipline, one more bartender at peak hours. Cheapest way to win back review score.
03
Turn "all-inclusive" from a trap into a gift
Make water and coffee free. Add a paid upgrade tier for the nice restaurant. Stops the "ripoff" reviews and makes more money at the same time.
04
Make the kosher resort actually Greek
Chalkida is now a kosher resort — good idea, weak execution. One review says: "I was in Greece, and there was no Greek salad!" A kosher-Greek menu is an easy, obvious fix and a real differentiator.
05
Build a Greece-only content page about food
Casa Cook gets 6x the followers by posting about food and drink. Brown Greece has almost no presence. A single Instagram/TikTok page focused on food, run properly, is the fastest way to build demand before 2027.
